# Directorate for Communications

In this OECD role, the **Directorate for Communications (COM)** is the central OECD department responsible for making sure the organisation's work is communicated clearly, consistently, and effectively.

Based on [raw_offer.txt](/Users/evadelmarperez/Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-PUIG/Python%20projects/Personal/intew/OECD%20comunications/raw_offer.txt), COM:

- maximises the reach and impact of OECD work
- works across all OECD directorates
- leads corporate media, social media, stakeholder and parliamentary engagement
- manages the organisation's online presence and digital content
- disseminates OECD publications
- monitors communications performance and reputational risk
- helps set communications standards across the organisation

In simple terms, it is the OECD's **central communications and communications-strategy function**.
