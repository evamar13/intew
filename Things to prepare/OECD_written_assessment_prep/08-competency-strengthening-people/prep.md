# Competency: Strengthening People

## What It Means At OECD

This competency is about creating conditions where people can perform, learn, improve, and contribute fully. In a written assessment for a junior role, you are unlikely to be judged as a formal people manager, but you can still demonstrate this competency indirectly.

## What You Can Show At Junior Level

- willingness to learn and improve
- respect for diverse contributions
- awareness of barriers that reduce team effectiveness
- a collaborative attitude that helps others perform well

## How To Demonstrate It In Writing

You can show this competency by recommending approaches that:

- make complex information easier for others to use
- help teams build shared capability
- remove obstacles to effective work
- support inclusion of different skills and perspectives
- create clarity, guidance, and feedback loops

## Example Angles

- propose dashboards or briefs that help non-specialists act confidently
- recommend simple methodological guidance for teams using new AI indicators
- suggest cross-team knowledge-sharing where measurement practices differ
- frame reporting as a capacity-building tool, not just a compliance output

## Strong Signals

- "The framework should be simple enough for communications colleagues across directorates to use consistently."
- "A pilot phase could include guidance notes and feedback loops so teams can adopt new indicators confidently."
- "Standardisation should support learning, not burden teams with unnecessary complexity."

## What Weak Performance Looks Like

- writing as if analysis exists only for your own use
- ignoring the needs of non-technical colleagues
- proposing systems that are overly complex or unrealistic
- failing to recognise that adoption depends on people, not just tools

## How To Sound Mature Without Overclaiming

Do not pretend to be a senior manager. Instead, sound like someone who:

- helps others succeed
- values constructive feedback
- thinks about usability
- notices process friction and wants to improve it

## Self-Check

- Did I make my recommendation usable by others?
- Did I consider different audiences and skill levels?
- Did I show openness to learning and improvement?
- Did I avoid sounding purely individualistic?
