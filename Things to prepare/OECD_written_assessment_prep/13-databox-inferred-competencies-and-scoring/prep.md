## How To Translate These Into Writing

### Written Proficiency

- write clearly
- structure the note well
- sound professional, not academic

### Analytical Thinking

- identify the real issue
- separate symptoms from causes
- synthesize evidence

### Policy Evaluation

- compare options
- show trade-offs
- make a justified recommendation

### Vision and Strategy

- connect the answer to OECD mission and longer-term impact

### Adaptability

- show comfort with AI-era uncertainty
- recommend a test-and-learn approach where appropriate

### Collaboration

- involve the right internal stakeholders
- avoid siloed proposals

### Achieve Results

- make the recommendation implementable
- sequence actions
- focus on useful outputs

## What A High-Scoring Answer Will Probably Feel Like

- concise
- analytical
- decision-oriented
- careful with evidence
- practical
- institutionally aware

## What A Weaker Answer Will Probably Feel Like

- generic
- descriptive rather than analytical
- technically enthusiastic without governance
- vague about next steps
- detached from OECD’s institutional context
