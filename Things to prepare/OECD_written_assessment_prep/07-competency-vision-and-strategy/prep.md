# Competency: Vision And Strategy

## What It Means At OECD

This competency is about understanding the OECD's mission, the wider global environment, and how your work supports long-term institutional objectives. It also includes using evidence to make sound decisions and anticipating change.

## What It Likely Means For A Junior Written Assessment

You are not being tested as a senior strategist. You are being tested on whether you can:

- understand the bigger purpose of the work
- connect your analysis to institutional priorities
- think beyond immediate metrics
- make sensible, evidence-based recommendations

## Level 1 Behaviors To Demonstrate In Writing

- show that you understand how the team objective supports OECD's mission
- consider external changes, especially AI and platform shifts
- use data and evidence when making claims
- show awareness of consequences of different choices

## How To Show This In An Essay

- explain why the issue matters beyond a narrow technical point
- connect recommendations to public-policy relevance, reach, and trust
- show that you understand stakeholder needs and future implications
- make decisions using reasoned criteria, not intuition alone

## Signals Of Stronger-than-Minimum Performance

- noticing emerging risks early
- balancing short-term needs with long-term value
- recognising trade-offs explicitly
- framing recommendations in strategic rather than purely operational terms

## Example Moves You Can Make

- "The OECD should assess not only whether its content is visible in AI systems, but whether that visibility supports accurate understanding of its evidence."
- "A useful reporting framework should preserve comparability with existing metrics while allowing experimental AI indicators to mature."

## Weak Signals To Avoid

- answering only at the tool level
- ignoring the OECD mission
- giving isolated recommendations with no strategic logic
- failing to distinguish urgent action from strategic direction

## Self-Check

- Did I connect my answer to the organisation's mission?
- Did I show awareness of external changes?
- Did I use evidence or logic to justify my recommendation?
- Did I show that I understand why the issue matters institutionally?
