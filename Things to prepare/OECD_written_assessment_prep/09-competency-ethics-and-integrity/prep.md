# Competency: Ethics And Integrity

## What It Means At OECD

This is central, not decorative. OECD links its competency framework to values, ethics, respect, confidentiality, impartiality, and accountability.

For this role, ethics and integrity also apply to:

- handling uncertain metrics responsibly
- avoiding misleading claims
- respecting diversity and inclusion
- protecting trust in OECD analysis

## What You Need To Show In Writing

- honesty about limitations
- balanced interpretation of data
- respect for different stakeholders
- rejection of careless or overstated claims
- sensitivity to reputational implications

## How This Applies To AI And Communications Analytics

You should be ready to show that:

- AI outputs may misrepresent evidence
- metrics can be noisy or incomplete
- analysis should not be manipulated to tell a convenient story
- new measurement methods require governance and transparency
- public institutions should be especially careful with credibility

## Good Ways To Demonstrate This Competency

- explicitly name uncertainties
- distinguish experimental indicators from validated reporting metrics
- mention fairness, inclusion, and responsible communication
- recommend transparent methodology and documentation
- avoid hype and false precision

## Useful Phrases

- "Findings should be presented with clear methodological caveats."
- "Any exploratory AI indicator should be documented transparently before being used for performance judgment."
- "Protecting the credibility of OECD analysis is as important as extending measurement coverage."

## What Weak Answers Look Like

- overclaiming what data shows
- treating experimental metrics as definitive
- ignoring ethics in AI-related measurement
- using dismissive or culturally insensitive language
- proposing aggressive optimisation without discussing trust or accuracy

## Self-Check

- Did I acknowledge uncertainty honestly?
- Did I avoid exaggerated claims?
- Did I show respect for diverse stakeholders?
- Did I protect institutional credibility in my recommendation?
