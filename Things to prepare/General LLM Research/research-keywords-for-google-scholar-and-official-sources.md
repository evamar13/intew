# Research Keywords for Google Scholar and Official Sources

This file is a reusable bank of search terms for finding academic publications, institutional reports, official documentation, and analytics sources related to LLM visibility, AI-mediated discovery, crawler behaviour, and AI-era measurement.

## How to search

Use these patterns in Google Scholar:

- `"exact phrase"`
- `OR` for variants
- `site:` only in normal Google, not Scholar
- `intitle:` in Google Scholar when useful
- add `2023`, `2024`, `2025`, `2026` when you want recent work
- add `pdf` in normal Google when you want downloadable reports

Useful suffixes to add:

- `measurement`
- `framework`
- `evaluation`
- `methodology`
- `case study`
- `dashboard`
- `referral traffic`
- `analytics`
- `observability`
