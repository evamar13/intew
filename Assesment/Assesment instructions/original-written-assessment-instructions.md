## Question 1

### Background

The OECD produces evidence-based analysis and policy advice for governments and policymakers around the world. As generative AI tools and Large Language Models (LLMs) become increasingly important sources of information, organisations are paying growing attention to their visibility within AI-generated responses.

You have been provided with a [*dataset*](https://storage.googleapis.com/maki-public/tiny_mce%2F3d2e5fe2-264a-4209-92c3-464559e5dbf3%2FExcel%20File%20supporting%20document_Question1_Junior%20AI%20%26%20Communication%20Officer%20REF3112J.xlsx) containing OECD visibility metrics from an LLM visibility monitoring platform (WriteSonic).

Your task is to prepare a concise, executive-ready synthesis for senior communications leaders.

The assessment is intentionally designed to allow and encourage the use of AI tools. We are interested not only in the final output, but also in how you use AI to support your work.

### Task

Using the materials provided and your own research, please prepare a short executive briefing answering the following question:

**How visible is the OECD in Large Language Models, what does the broader evidence tell us about this emerging field, and what actions should the OECD consider?**

You are expected to use AI tools to support your analysis, research, writing, visualisation and presentation design.
