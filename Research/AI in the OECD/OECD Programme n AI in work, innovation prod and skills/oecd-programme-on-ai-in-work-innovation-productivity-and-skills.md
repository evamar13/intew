## Programme Description

The OECD programme on AI in Work, Innovation, Productivity and Skills (AI-WIPS), with the support of Germany, analyses the impact of AI on the labour market, skills and social policy.

## Initiative Overview

The OECD is working with governments around the world to measure and analyse the impact of AI on training needs and labour markets. This programme is intended to help governments shape AI-related policies that are responsible and human-centred, and that improve individual and societal well-being.

Last year's AI-WIPS Conference, the OECD International Conference on AI in Work, Innovation, Productivity, and Skills, took place on `12 December 2024` and `13 December 2024`.

The `2023` and `2024` International Conferences on AI in Work, Innovation, Productivity, and Skills brought together leading voices from the policy, academic, business, technical and civil society communities to discuss:

- how AI affects employment
- how AI affects skills
- how AI affects productivity
- how AI affects innovation
- how policymaking can respond

Alongside the implications of ChatGPT-like tools, participants discussed:

- new OECD evidence on the impact of AI in the workplace and education
- whether AI is the solution to skills shortages
- risk-based approaches for AI governance
- broader implications for labour markets and societies

AI is widely expected to:

- complement humans in some tasks
- replace humans in other tasks
- generate new types of work
- transform how people organise and carry out work today

Governments around the world are therefore faced with managing the AI transition and ensuring that AI is developed and deployed across economies and societies in a way that is:

- responsible
- human-centred
- beneficial to individual and societal well-being

The OECD AI Principles call policymakers' attention to a critical policy area: building human capacity and preparing for a fair labour market transformation.

The OECD is now working to inform policymakers in implementing the AI Principles through the OECD AI Policy Observatory, `OECD.AI`.

Germany's support enables OECD.AI to host a dedicated Programme on AI in Work, Innovation, Productivity and Skills (AI-WIPS). This collaboration aims to produce:

- in-depth analysis
- measurement
- opportunities for international dialogue
- concrete policy assessments on how AI impacts labour markets and societies

Through collaboration with:

- international policy communities
- research communities
- business communities
- labour representatives
- civil society

the OECD seeks to identify necessary employment, skills and social policy reforms.

The AI-WIPS programme also benefits from synergies with the wider programme of work and policy communities that make up the OECD AI Policy Observatory.

With the German AI Observatory launched by the Policy Lab Digital, Work and Society of the Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs, Germany is promoting:

- public understanding of AI
- trust in AI
- acceptance of AI
- people-centred AI policies for a changing world of work
