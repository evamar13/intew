## Appointment Note

Camille Raymond shared that she was officially appointed as Head of the Communications Impact, Standards & Capacity Building Division (CISC) at the OECD.

She noted that she had already been serving in the role in an acting capacity for the previous two years and expressed gratitude for the opportunity to continue the work with the CISC team and the COM management team, including Meenu, Maria, Carol, and Julian, under the leadership of Stephane Levesque.

- strengthening communication standards

- supporting impactful strategies
