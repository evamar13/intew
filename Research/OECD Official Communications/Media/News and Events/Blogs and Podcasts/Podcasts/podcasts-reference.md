## Podcast Positioning Text

The page frames OECD podcasts as short, insight-led interviews:

- approximately `15 minutes`
- interviews with OECD and guest experts
- topics include inequality and inclusive growth, digital transformation, social change, the environment, international co-operation, and more

## Communications Value

- more accessible than long reports
- personality-driven and voice-led
- well suited to recurrent audience engagement
- useful for issue explanation and thought leadership
