## Video Item Explicitly Mentioned

## Summary Given On The Page

Disparities in employment rates between regions within OECD countries exceed 20 percentage points in more than half of OECD economies, with large and persistent gaps in employment, unemployment and disposable incomes. The video takes stock of the current landscape and proposes next steps for public action.
