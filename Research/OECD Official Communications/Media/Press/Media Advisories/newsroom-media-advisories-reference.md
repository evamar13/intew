# Newsroom Media Advisories Reference

## Function

Media advisories are pre-announcement items. They alert journalists to an upcoming OECD release, event, or presentation before the main press or publication drop.

- `OECD to present latest results from Survey on Drivers of Trust in Public Institutions on Monday 29 June` - `24 June 2026`
- `OECD to launch the Economic Survey of Korea on 2 July` - `23 June 2026`

## Why This Matters Operationally

This is a classic newsroom function:

- build advance awareness
- prepare press attendance or coverage
- sync embargo handling with launch timing
- drive attendance to virtual or in-person events
