# Newsroom Broadcast And Studio Services Reference

## Function

The OECD Newsroom offers operational support for broadcasters through in-house TV and radio studio facilities.

## Contact Points

- General information: `news.contact@oecd.org`
- Studio bookings: `COM.registrations@oecd.org`

## TV Facilities And Services

- TV studio fully equipped with Balcar fluo lighting and OECD decor
- Satellite feeds via Globecast
- Booking of space segment to be made by receiver: `Paris Serte +33 1 44 61 47 00`
- IFB available for incoming questions: `OECD IFB +33 1 45 24 81 58`
- Coordination and talk-back: `+33 1 45 24 80 98`
- Panasonic P2 HD video cameras
- Studio output: `HD-SDI 1080i PAL`
- Facilities, camera, sound, and technical assistance available to broadcasters free of charge

## Radio Facilities And Services

- Live or pre-recorded interviews can be conducted in the studio
- Phone lines supported
- Specialized radio circuits supported
- ISDN digital lines supported
- Radio studio with ISDN line: `+33 1 44 14 65 35 - G722/H320`

## Classification Note

This is press infrastructure, not only content. It belongs under `Media/Press` because it supports media production and live coverage.
