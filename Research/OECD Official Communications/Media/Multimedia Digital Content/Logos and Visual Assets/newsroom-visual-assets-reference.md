# Newsroom Visual Assets Reference

## Visual Asset Categories Explicitly Mentioned

- OECD Ministerial Council Meeting 2026 photos
- OECD Headquarters photos
- official OECD logos

## Usage Conditions And Handling

- multimedia gallery content is presented for media use
- gallery notes a `Creative Commons licence CC BY`
- journalists can download and use high resolution photos for print or web
- video can be linked or embedded as appropriate
- OECD copyright should be included
- the photographer should be credited

## Contact Points For Asset Questions

- photo issues or missing images: `COM.registrations@oecd.org`
- permissions and rights management: `comrights@oecd.org`

## Interpretation

The Newsroom treats logos, photos, and visual identity as controlled communications assets that support external coverage while preserving attribution and rights discipline.
