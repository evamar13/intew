# Newsroom Multimedia Gallery Reference

## Function

The multimedia gallery is the OECD's media-facing repository for reusable visual assets.

## Positioning

- section label: `Multimedia gallery`
- purpose statement: `Here you can find essential visual resources for media.`
- licence note: `Creative Commons licence CC BY`

## Featured Collections Mentioned

- `OECD Ministerial Council Meeting 2026`
- `OECD Headquarters`
- `Logos`

## What The Gallery Offers

- downloadable images from the OECD Ministerial Council Meeting 2026
- downloadable images of OECD Headquarters
- access to official OECD logos
- visual identity guidance through the logo access link

## Broader Multimedia Support Mentioned In Newsroom Material

- high resolution photos for print or web
- ability to link to or embed video as appropriate
- OECD copyright should be included
- photographer should be credited
- videos can be browsed on `www.youtube.com/oecd`

## Classification Note

This belongs under `Media/Multimedia Digital Content` because it is a reusable asset bank for external media use.
