# Newsroom Media Audience Reference

## Why This Sits Under Audiences

The OECD Newsroom is built first for media-facing users, especially journalists and broadcasters who need fast access to OECD announcements, embargoed reports, experts, visual assets, and event information.

## Primary Audience Segments

- Accredited journalists who want advance notice of OECD events, reports, and statistics
- Press and newsroom staff covering OECD policy areas
- Broadcast media using OECD studio facilities
- Regional media, including Latin America coverage through the OECD Mexico Centre
- Journalists looking for embargoed publications, experts, conferences, and media events

## What The Newsroom Gives This Audience

- Search for relevant news by topic, country, or event
- Media registration for advance notice
- Press releases
- Media advisories
- Press launches
- Statistical news releases
- Speeches and statements
- Press contacts by policy beat
- Embargo access workflow
- Multimedia gallery, photos, video, and logos
- TV and radio studio support

## Audience Logic

This is not a general-public information architecture only. It is a specialist media service layer inside OECD communications.

The clearest signals are:

- explicit journalist accreditation
- embargo rules
- press-by-beat contact allocation
- broadcaster facilities
- media-use guidance for photos, video, and rights
