## About OECD Publications

The OECD is one of the world's largest and most reliable sources of internationally comparable socio-economic and environmental statistics. As well as collecting data from its Members and partner countries, OECD monitors trends, analyses and forecasts economic developments, and researches social changes or evolving patterns, publishing over 1 250 titles annually. By making these freely accessible to all on this platform, OECD is playing a major role in enabling better policies for better lives.

## OECD Open Access Policy

Universal access to reliable and relevant information is key to supporting citizens' participation in the democratic process and ensuring better policies for better lives. The OECD is committed to supporting information integrity and accessibility. Its policy on open access seeks to ensure that, as a fundamental part of its mission, the published outputs of its activities are freely accessible and reusable by the public, with the goal of increasing their accessibility, visibility and impact.

## How To Use?

### Searching and finding content

### Browse and navigate

OECD publications can be easily discovered in a number of ways. For those wishing to explore content, titles have been organised by content category, date of publication or policy area/s covered.

From the navigation bar you can access the full lists of our publications by 2 content categories: Reports and research papers and Policy papers and briefs. Filters are available on the left-hand side of each of the lists to refine the selection displayed.

The publications page shows our featured titles, and the most recent and forthcoming titles by date of publication.

The website is organised around more than 200 topics. Each topic page shows most relevant titles in easy to browse lists.

The citation feature in Publications allows you to easily generate citations for publications available in the repository to help you properly reference OECD materials in your work. Click on the "Cite this publication" button typically found on the right-hand side of the publication's page.

### KBART files

KBART (Knowledge Bases and Related Tools) are standardised files that facilitate the efficient exchange of metadata between content providers and knowledge bases. KBART files are available in TXT format and updated monthly. A single zip file can be downloaded by any visitor to the site, and each one contains the complete set of monographs (ISBNs) and serials (ISSNs), as well as sub-sets, broken down into one of OECD's 20 topics.

### MARC records

MARC (Machine-Readable Cataloging) records enable the integration and management of OECD publications' metadata in libraries cataloguing systems. The OECD's are available as MARC21 XML and updated monthly. A single zip file can be downloaded by any visitor to the site, and each one contains the changes for books and papers made since the previous month, including all creations, modifications and deletions. In addition, a complete set of records is provided.
