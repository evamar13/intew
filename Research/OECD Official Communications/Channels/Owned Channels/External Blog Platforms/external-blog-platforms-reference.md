# OECD External Blog Platforms Reference

## Why These Sit Under Owned Channels

These platforms are OECD-branded editorial environments even when they are presented as distinct blog properties.

## Platforms Listed On The Page

- `Development Matters`
- `Ecoscope`
- `OECD Education and skills today`
- `Observatory of Public Sector Innovation (OPSI)`
- `The SDG Communicator`
- `OECD Cogito`
- `OECD Skills and Work`
- `The AI Wonk`

## Communications Role

These platforms allow the OECD to:

- segment editorial voice by topic or community
- maintain recurring commentary formats
- publish thought leadership outside formal publication workflows
- build repeat audiences around specialised subject areas
