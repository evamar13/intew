In an era where artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping societies, economies, and governance, understanding and comparing national and international AI policies has become more critical than ever. That's why OECD.AI has launched its AI policy navigator, a redesigned and expanded tool to track public AI policies and initiatives worldwide.

[>> Visit the OECD.AI Policy Navigator <<](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/overview)

A centralised, global repository for everyone

The Policy Navigator replaces the previous OECD.AI policy database with a more intuitive and powerful platform. Designed in consultation with its primary audience, national contact points and policy users, it simplifies the process of tracking and submitting AI policy information, while increasing coverage and usability.

New features include:

- More initiatives, more sources: the Policy Navigator covers over 1,300 initiatives from more than 80 jurisdictions and international organisations.
- Faster updates: updates are now published more quickly, and users can view the last update time for each entry.
- More intuitive interface: improved filters, layout, and accessibility provide policymakers and researchers with faster access to relevant AI policies and initiatives.

A tool for global collaboration

The Policy Navigator is more than a reference library. It's a live tool co-created and maintained by global experts, helping countries and institutions learn from one another as they shape the future of responsible AI. It allows policymakers to identify global trends, track policy diffusion, and benchmark their initiatives.
