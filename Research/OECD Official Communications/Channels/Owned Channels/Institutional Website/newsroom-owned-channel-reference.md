# Newsroom As Owned Channel Reference

## Why This Sits Under Owned Channels

The Newsroom is part of the OECD institutional website. OECD owns the platform, the publishing workflow, the information architecture, and the release timing.

## Main Site Components Visible In The Newsroom Material

- `Newsroom`
- `Contact`
- `Multimedia gallery`
- language switching: `English`, `francais`, `espanol`
- topic, country, or event search
- media registration
- featured stories
- latest press releases
- upcoming media advisories
- press launches
- statistical news releases
- speeches and statements
- related links to events, networks, and contact/social pages

## Channel Role

The Newsroom is an owned distribution layer for:

- media relations
- announcement publishing
- release scheduling
- media-service operations
- visual asset distribution

## Distinction

The Newsroom is not just a content page. It is also a workflow hub for press access, embargo handling, and media support.
