# Blogs, Videos and Podcasts As Owned Channel Reference

## Why This Sits Under Owned Channels

The main `Blogs, videos & podcasts` page is part of the OECD website and is therefore an owned channel.

OECD controls:

- the page structure
- editorial selection
- featured placement
- language presentation
- navigation to internal and external OECD editorial properties

## What This Owned Channel Does

- highlights featured blog content
- surfaces latest blogs, podcasts, and videos
- routes audiences to podcast distribution platforms
- routes audiences to OECD external blog brands

## Difference From Newsroom

The `Newsroom` is media-service oriented.

The `Blogs, videos & podcasts` page is editorial and audience-engagement oriented.
