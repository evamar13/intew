## Summary

New data show that more than one-third of individuals across the OECD used generative AI tools in `2025`, highlighting how rapidly artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life. Uptake remains uneven across population groups, according to the OECD ICT Access and Usage Database.

## Main Findings On Individual Use

The largest divide is by age, with a gap of `53.6` percentage points. Differences by educational attainment and income level are also significant, both at around `21` percentage points. By contrast, the gender gap in AI use is relatively small, at `4.2` percentage points.

Usage is particularly high among students:

- `75%` of students aged 16 and over reported use of generative AI tools

Adoption is also widespread among people connected to the labour market:

- people in employment: `41.1%`
- unemployed people: `36.7%`

By contrast, retired and other inactive groups report much lower usage:

- retired and other inactive groups: `12.5%`

## Main Findings On Firm Adoption

Alongside rising use by individuals, AI adoption by firms continued to expand across OECD countries where data are available.

In `2025`:

- `20.2%` of firms reported using AI

This is up from:

- `14.2%` in `2024`
- `8.7%` in `2023`

This means adoption more than doubled over the previous two years.

Growth in firm-level AI use reached:

- `42.4%` in `2025`

This represents a moderation compared with earlier years.

## Country Pattern Mentioned

AI uptake surpassed `35%` in several Nordic countries, including:

- Denmark
- Finland
- Sweden

## Firm Size Gap

Adoption remains highly uneven across firms of different sizes:

- large firms using AI: `52.0%`
- small firms using AI: `17.4%`

The spread of general-purpose generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Copilot, widely available since `2024`, may have contributed to this increase.

## Industry Pattern Mentioned

In `2025`, `57.3%` of ICT firms across the OECD used AI, the highest share of any industry, followed by:

- professional and scientific services: `36.8%`

In some countries, AI use in the ICT sector has reached near-saturation levels:

- Sweden: `87.9%`
- Austria: `79.9%`
- Finland: `79.8%`

This leaves limited scope for further expansion in those cases.

By contrast, growth remained particularly strong in industries that had previously lagged behind:

- accommodation and food services: `62.5%` year-on-year growth
- construction: `59.1%` year-on-year growth

Other industries also recorded positive but more moderate growth rates ranging from `25.8%` to `51.7%`.
