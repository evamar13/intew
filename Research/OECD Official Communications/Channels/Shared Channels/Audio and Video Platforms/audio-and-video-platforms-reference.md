# Audio And Video Platforms Reference

## Why These Sit Under Shared Channels

The OECD can publish and distribute content on these platforms, but it does not own the platforms themselves.

They are third-party environments used for reach, discoverability, and audience convenience.

## Platforms Listed On The Page

- `Amazon podcasts`
- `Apple podcasts`
- `Deezer`
- `SoundCloud`
- `Spotify`
- `YouTube`

## Communications Role

These platforms support:

- podcast distribution
- video distribution
- listener and viewer convenience
- audience growth outside the OECD website
- platform-native discovery

## Classification Note

In this context, `YouTube` is treated primarily as an audio-video distribution platform rather than only as a social network.
