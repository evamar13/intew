# Newsroom Social Distribution Reference

## Why This Sits Under Shared Channels

The Newsroom explicitly points users from the OECD website toward platform-dependent social environments where distribution, discovery, and engagement take place on third-party systems.

## Social And Distribution Platforms Mentioned

- LinkedIn
- X
- Facebook
- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- YouTube

## Roles These Platforms Play In The Newsroom Context

- amplify OECD content after publication
- distribute multimedia assets and clips
- extend reach beyond the OECD website
- support engagement with journalists and broader audiences

## Website Signals

The Newsroom material explicitly says users can:

- engage with OECD social media content on `LinkedIn`, `X`, `Facebook`, `Instagram`, and `WhatsApp`
- browse OECD videos on `www.youtube.com/oecd`

## Classification Note

These are not owned channels. OECD controls the accounts and content strategy, but the platforms themselves are third-party distribution environments, so they belong in `Shared Channels`.
