This folder groups the main OECD.AI dashboard entry points you highlighted.

The `National` section focuses on country-level strategies, initiatives, and policies.

The `International` section focuses on AI initiatives developed by or within international organisations and fora.

The `All AI initiatives and policies` section is the broadest searchable policy inventory.
