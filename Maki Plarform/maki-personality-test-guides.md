# Maki Personality Test Guides

## Scope

This document summarizes two personality-test help articles referenced in Maki materials:

- the `Big 5` test
- the `MPI-6F` test

## The Big 5 test

### What it is

Maki describes the Big 5 as a descriptive personality model built around five fundamental dimensions:

- Extraversion
- Agreeableness
- Openness
- Conscientiousness
- Neuroticism

Each dimension is described as a continuum between two extremes rather than a fixed category.

### How results are computed

For each dimension, a candidate is assigned to one of three groups:

- `Low`
- `Medium`
- `High`

This grouping is described as based on a representative sample study, referred to as a calibrated cohort.

### Interpretation example

For `Extraversion`, the article gives the following interpretation:

- `Low`: introverted, solitary, and reserved
- `Medium`: ambivert, engaging, and flexible
- `High`: outgoing, lively, and sociable

### Where to find the results

Maki states that recruiters can access the results:

- on the candidate’s detailed results page
- in the `Personality traits` section
- in the broader candidate assessment report accessible from the three-dot menu near the overall score

## The MPI-6F test

### What it is

Maki describes `MPI-6F` (also referred to as `CISDAG`) as a version derived from the HEXACO model.

The six dimensions are presented as professional-context labels for the underlying framework:

- Emotional Stability
- Gregariousness
- Diligence
- Amicableness
- Open-mindedness
- Truthfulness-Humbleness

### How results are computed

As with the Big 5 guide, Maki explains that candidates are assigned to one of three groups per dimension:

- `Low`
- `Medium`
- `High`

This grouping is also described as based on a representative sample study or calibrated cohort.

### Interpretation example

The article uses an Extraversion-style example for interpretation:

- `Low`: introverted, solitary, and reserved
- `Medium`: ambivert, engaging, and flexible
- `High`: outgoing, lively, and sociable

### Where to find the results

Maki says recruiters can find MPI-6F results:

- on the candidate’s detailed page
- in the `Personality traits` section
- in the candidate evaluation report accessible from the three-dot menu at the top right of the overall score

## Shared interpretation model

Across both articles, the common interpretation logic is:

- the tests position the candidate relative to personality dimensions
- the output is grouped into low, medium, and high ranges
- the grouping is based on calibration rather than an absolute pass/fail logic
