# OECD Recruitment Data Notice In The Maki Context

## Scope

This document summarizes the parts of the OECD recruitment data-protection notice that are relevant to Maki’s role in OECD hiring processes.

## OECD recruitment system context

The OECD states that its recruitment process is managed through:

- `SmartRecruiters`, hosted in the United States

The OECD notice explains that this system is used for:

- candidate management
- job applications
- internal and external recruitment processes

## Data collected by the OECD

The notice states that the OECD collects information provided when applying, including:

- name
- email address
- place of residence
- nationality
- gender
- education
- work history

The notice also explicitly states that applicants are not asked to provide information about:

- racial or ethnic origin
- sexual orientation
- political opinions or activities
- philosophical beliefs
- union membership
- religious beliefs

## Where Maki appears in the process

The OECD notice states that candidates may be asked to complete:

- prerecorded video interviews
- written assessments

through an external provider identified as `Maki`.

This places Maki as one of the external vendors used by the OECD within the recruitment process.

## Other external providers mentioned

The notice also references:

- `Cubiks`, for certain high-level evaluation processes
- `DocuSign`, for electronic signature of offer-related documents
- `AssessFirst`, specifically for the Young Associates Programme

## Retention under the OECD notice

The OECD states that, unless the candidate explicitly asks for deletion, candidate information remains in the OECD candidate database for:

- `7 years`

It also states that:

- candidates can request deletion through the Candidate Tracking Portal
- candidates can also request deletion via `recruitment@oecd.org`

For candidates placed on a shortlist group (`Group A`), the notice states that the application may be considered for similar roles during the `2 years` following committee approval.

## Candidate rights under the OECD notice

The OECD notice states that candidates have the right to:

- access personal data
- rectify personal data
- object to processing
- request erasure
- challenge a decision based on an AI system that harms them

Contact provided in the notice:

- `recruitment@oecd.org`

## Why this matters for the Maki folder

This notice is relevant because it clarifies the external institutional context in which Maki may be used:

- Maki is part of OECD’s recruitment vendor chain for some interviews and written assessments
- the OECD remains the entity describing the broader recruitment-data process
- candidate rights and retention in the OECD context are not identical to Maki’s own generic platform policies
