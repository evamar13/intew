# Maki Trust, Security, And Fairness

## Core position

Maki presents itself as a decision-support platform built on validated assessment science, enterprise-grade security, ethical AI, and explainable scoring.

Two key claims define this positioning:

- every score is anchored in validated assessment science
- hiring decisions remain human-led, with no automatic rejection by the system

## How hiring decisions are made

Maki explicitly states that it is `decision-support, not decision-making`.

Important operational principles:

- there is no automatic-rejection feature
- a Maki score cannot automatically disqualify a candidate
- any adverse decision requires human review of the full results
- meaningful human intervention must come from someone able to change the decision
- every score is tied to observable behavior
- full transcripts and rationales support traceability

This positioning is linked to GDPR Article 22 considerations around automated decision-making.

## Secure infrastructure

Maki describes its infrastructure with four main pillars:

- `Encryption`: end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest
- `Hosting`: cloud-based, GDPR-compliant data centers
- `Privacy-first design`: data minimization and purpose limitation
- `Monitoring`: continuous performance monitoring and alerts

## Bias resistance and fairness

Maki’s fairness claims include:

- annual independent bias audits by Holistic AI under NYC Local Law 144
- science-backed assessments designed with industrial-organizational psychology principles
- structured, standardized evaluations to reduce subjectivity
- explainable scoring with clear rationales
- accessibility and accommodations built into the assessment experience

Accommodation and accessibility claims include:

- WCAG 2.1 AA alignment
- reasonable adjustments on request
- extra time when needed
- written alternatives to spoken tasks
- no requirement that every candidate complete one single experience format

Maki also emphasizes `content-only scoring`, meaning evaluation is focused on the substance of the response rather than delivery style, speaking speed, or speech patterns. Scoring is described as anchored to `Behaviourally Anchored Rating Scales (BARS)`.

## Ethical AI standards

Maki frames its ethical AI posture around:

- `Transparency`: agent decision processes documented
- `Human oversight`: no automatic rejection
- `Continuous improvement`: feedback loops used to retrain models safely
- `Accountability`: input from external advisors including Josh Bersin Company, OpenAI, ex-Meta HR, and Salesforce

## Candidate experience and accessibility

Maki’s candidate experience claims focus on accessibility and reach:

- mobile-first design
- web and phone access
- multilingual coverage across `25+ languages`
- accommodation requests and alternative formats supported
- strong candidate perception impact, with claims of up to `99%` improved candidate perception
