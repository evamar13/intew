# Maki And Recruitics Partnership

## Partnership summary

Maki announced a partnership with Recruitics to bring AI-powered voice interviewing into `AdaptiveApply`, Recruitics’ candidate experience technology and hiring-solutions marketplace.

The central idea is to embed Maki’s voice screening inside the pre-apply flow so that screening can happen before an application reaches the ATS.

## What the partnership changes

The integration allows employers to:

- embed Maki’s AI voice interviews directly into the application flow
- assess candidates before the formal ATS stage
- deliver pre-qualified candidate signals to recruiters
- keep the application process more seamless for candidates

Maki frames this as moving assessment earlier in the journey, rather than waiting until after the application is complete.

## Why this matters

The partnership is positioned as a response to two problems:

- generative AI has made résumés easier to optimize and less reliable as a quality signal
- some qualified or passive candidates may not have a current résumé available

By introducing voice screening earlier, Maki and Recruitics argue that employers can evaluate:

- skills
- experience
- communication
- fit

without making a polished or updated résumé a prerequisite.

## Key benefits highlighted

- stronger candidate signal through live adaptive voice interaction
- lower application friction
- broader access to talent
- improved recruiter efficiency
- more defensible decisions through validated criteria and human final review

## Mochi’s role in the partnership

The partnership centers on `Mochi`, Maki’s Voice Screening capability.

Mochi is described as:

- a real adaptive voice conversation
- available `24/7`
- delivered in dozens of languages
- designed to evaluate every candidate against validated, science-backed criteria
- able to produce a ranked and defensible shortlist quickly

The system is still framed as supporting, not replacing, human decision-making.

## Target hiring contexts

The partnership is pitched particularly for large-scale hiring environments, including:

- high-volume frontline roles
- retail
- warehouses
- contact centers
- delivery fleets
- frontline healthcare
- large enterprises hiring across many roles at once

## Positioning from each company

Maki’s side of the message is that Recruitics attracts the right people efficiently, while Maki screens them fairly at scale by voice.

Recruitics’ side of the message is that employers gain direct candidate signal inside the apply flow, helping them move beyond résumé-heavy screening and toward more authentic candidate evidence.

## Broader company positioning included in the announcement

The announcement also restates Maki’s broader operating model:

- Hiring Intelligence across five capabilities
- built on top of existing ATS environments
- validated assessment science
- defensible hiring decisions
- enterprise readiness through ISO 27001, GDPR, and EU AI Act readiness
- operation across `60+ countries` and `45+ languages`

It also repeats several commercial claims:

- up to `25%` lower early-tenure turnover
- `3x` faster time-to-hire
- `90%` of screening and assessment work automated
- `98%` candidate NPS

## Strategic takeaway

The Recruitics partnership positions Maki not just as an assessment vendor, but as a voice-based screening layer that can move high-signal evaluation upstream into the earliest part of candidate conversion.
