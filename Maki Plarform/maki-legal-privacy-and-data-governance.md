# Maki Legal, Privacy, And Data Governance

## Scope

This document consolidates the main legal and data-governance points from Maki’s:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Sale
- Terms of Use
- Personal Data Transfer Agreement

It focuses on governance, roles, rights, retention, transfers, and contractual structure rather than product claims already covered elsewhere.

## Published entity information in source documents

The source materials reference different legal entities and publication details, including:

- `MakiPeople SAS`, 37 Avenue Trudaine, 75009 Paris, in the Privacy Policy
- `LLC Technologie`, 5 Rue d'Assas, 75006 Paris, in the Terms of Sale and Terms of Use
- Trade Register number `899 882 989` in the Terms of Sale and Terms of Use

## Core platform purpose

Maki’s legal documents describe the platform as a service used to:

- run assessments and tests
- support recruitment processes
- help employers evaluate candidates
- support internal talent and career-development use cases

## Data-controller and processor roles

The documents distinguish between multiple roles:

### Maki as data controller

Maki states that it acts as data controller for processing done for its own purposes, such as:

- operating and improving the platform
- securing the services
- managing accounts
- communicating with users
- complying with legal obligations
- handling data-subject requests
- managing disputes

### Maki as data processor

Maki may also process data on behalf of employers or recruiters when delivering services. In that context, it describes itself as a data processor for certain activities.

### Independent-controller relationship with clients

The transfer agreement also states that Maki and the client may act as `independent data controllers`, especially when candidate data is transferred between them.

The documents mention several legal bases, including:

- performance of a contract
- legitimate interests
- consent
- explicit consent for special-category data where required
- legal obligations

## Cookies and tracking

The Privacy Policy states that Maki uses cookies and similar technologies for:

- platform functionality
- preference retention
- understanding platform use

It distinguishes between:

- strictly necessary cookies
- non-essential cookies subject to consent where required

The policy also notes a `13-month` consent validity period for accepted cookies.

It references additional opt-out links related to online data partners and GDPR-related advertising/data collection opt-out.

## Retention

The Privacy Policy states that data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described.

Examples explicitly mentioned:

- account information: while the account is active and up to `2 years` after last login or account deletion
- non-essential cookies: while consent is valid and no longer than the applicable consent period

## Data sharing

The Privacy Policy states that personal data may be shared with:

- service providers
- employers and recruiters tied to the relevant assessment
- advisors such as lawyers and accountants
- buyers/successors in corporate transactions
- public authorities or courts where legally required

The policy also states that Maki may share candidate results, analytics, feedback, and certain assessment snapshots with employers and recruiters tied to the process.

## International transfers

Maki states that some service providers may be located outside the European Union, or may process data outside the EU.

The policy says such transfers are carried out with safeguards intended to meet applicable data-protection requirements.

Contact for more information on safeguards:

- `privacy@makipeople.com`

## Data-subject rights

The Privacy Policy lists rights including:

- access
- rectification
- deletion
- portability
- restriction
- objection
- complaint to the competent authority
- withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis
- post-mortem processing instructions

Rights requests are directed to:

- `privacy@makipeople.com`

## Security commitments

Maki states that it implements organizational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, destruction, and misuse.

The transfer agreement gives more operational examples, including:

- staff training
- confidentiality obligations
- access controls
- password policies
- malware protection
- update and patching practices
- firewalls and interface protections
- deletion and destruction controls

## Terms of Use highlights

The Terms of Use describe:

- acceptance by using or registering on the platform
- user obligations to provide truthful information
- restrictions on unlawful or abusive content
- platform use for assessments and hiring-related services
- notice that the platform uses AI
- confirmation that outputs are decision-support only
- employer responsibility for final decisions

## Terms of Sale highlights

The Terms of Sale focus on the client relationship and include:

- subscription structure
- candidate assessment credits
- auto-renewal rules
- suspension or termination conditions
- intellectual-property ownership
- invoice and payment conditions
- liability limitations
- client licensing restrictions
- service evolution and pricing changes

## Important legal positioning

Across the legal documents, Maki consistently frames itself as:

- a platform provider
- a service that supports, not replaces, employer decision-making
- a system whose outputs assist recruiters and employers
- a service with layered controller/processor relationships depending on the processing context
